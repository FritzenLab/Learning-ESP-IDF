1) "Libraries"/packages (component registry) for ESP32:

https://components.espressif.com/

Components names will go (have to be added) into the "idf_component.yml" file 
inside the "mail" folder of the project (alongside "main.c")
---
2) If something goes wrong with compilation, you can try do a:

idf.py fullclean
---
3) To actually compile the project:

idf.py build
---
4) To open monitor terminal (similar to Arduino serial monitor,but with ESP_LOGI()):

ESP-IDF: Monitor Device
---
